# Working on Voices of the North

1. Read your [GitHub issue](https://github.com/phatdat220707/voices-of-the-north/milestone/1), its [work item](docs/techathon/WORK-ITEMS.md), and the relevant code. Check the latest `main` and your working tree. Before editing, state your scope, the first result you can verify, and any handoff you need. Dat coordinates integration.
2. Start with the independent work listed for your issue. Use a local fixture or mock based on the [demo flow and shared interfaces](docs/techathon/DEMO-FLOW.md). Agree with the affected owners before changing a shared interface or file. If a dependency blocks the final test, record the exact handoff you need and keep working on the parts you can verify locally.
3. Branch from the latest `main` into a task-specific branch and use an isolated local environment. Keep your PR within the issue scope and link the issue. Use `Refs #N` for partial work and `Closes #N` when the issue's acceptance criteria are met. For a defect outside your scope, record how to reproduce it and route it to its owner.
4. Edit frontend source in `frontend/src/` and regenerate the bundle with `python tools/build_frontend.py`. If another PR changes the bundle first, update your branch and regenerate it from the combined source.
5. Verify locally first, then run a representative case on staging when the relevant interfaces are available. In the PR, list the commit, environment, expected and observed result, and outstanding checks. Distinguish fixtures, real services, and shared staging evidence. Send the handoff to the named reviewer. Dat coordinates merging and changes to shared environments.
6. Follow the [team update rules](docs/techathon/ROADMAP.md#team-updates). Get Dat's approval before enabling paid services or publishing a release with contributor access.

This repository and its issues are public. Keep credentials, invite lists, private links, internal discussions, raw user recordings, and identifying transcripts out of commits and issue evidence. Keep the bundled role-play fixtures and app asset paths intact. Historical documents are references. Use the issue and roadmap for current requirements.
